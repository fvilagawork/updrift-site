Delete your data

Updrift does not ask for your name or email. The game signs you in with a random player id, and keeps your progress, rounds, scores and purchase records under that id.

Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Updrift and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Reset progress, then Reset everything.
  3. The next time your phone is online, the game deletes everything our server holds for your player id (your save, rounds, scores, round numbers and purchase records) and then deletes the player id itself.

This cannot be undone. The game then starts again as a new player.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the game, email francisvilaga@melioranexa.com with the subject "Delete my Updrift data". Because we do not know who you are, deleting from inside the game is the surest way; by email we may need details from your device to find your data.

What is kept

Copies in our providers' backups and logs disappear within 30 days. Deleting your data does not cancel or refund a purchase. Records kept by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Google AdMob and Sentry are kept under their own policies.
