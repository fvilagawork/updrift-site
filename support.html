Support

Need help with Updrift? Email francisvilaga@melioranexa.com. We usually reply within a few days.

My progress is gone

Your progress is saved on your phone and on our server. If you reset progress in Settings, or delete and reinstall the game on Android, the game starts as a new player and the old progress cannot be brought back.

I bought something and did not get it

Coins and items arrive a few seconds after the store confirms the payment. Make sure your phone is online and open the game again. Balloons, the Starter Pack and the Bonus Pass can be restored in the shop with Restore purchases. If something is still missing, email us with the date of the purchase and what you bought.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple or Google. Ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone and iPad) or in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions, then Budget and history (Android).

Ads

Updrift has rewarded ads only, for players 13 and over, and only when you choose to watch one. Players under 13 never see ads.

See the privacy policy, or delete your data.
