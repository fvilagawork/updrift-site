Updrift privacy policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Updrift ("Updrift", "the game") collects, why, who else handles it, and what you can do about it. Updrift is made by Francis Irvin Vilaga ("we", "us"). You can reach us at francisvilaga@melioranexa.com.

We wrote this in plain words on purpose. If anything is unclear, email us.

The short version

  • You do not make an account. The game signs you in with a random id. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or address.
  • We keep your game progress and your finished rounds on our servers in Japan, so your progress is saved and scores can be checked.
  • The game asks for your birth year once, to decide which features fit your age.
  • Players under 13 never see ads, and the ad service is never started for them.
  • Players 13 and over can choose to watch rewarded ads from Google AdMob. They are asked for consent where the law requires it.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details.
  • You can delete everything we hold about you from inside the game: Settings, then Reset progress.
  • We do not sell your information.

What we collect and why

A random player id

The first time the game goes online, it signs you in anonymously. Our server provider gives you a random id (a long string of letters and numbers). It is not linked to your name, email or phone number. The sign-in key for this id is kept in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore).

We use it to keep your saved progress apart from everyone else's and to check that requests to our server come from you.

Your game progress

Your save holds things like your coins, level, XP, Stars, skills, the balloons, birds and skies you own, missions, the daily gift, badges, your best scores, your settings (sound, music, buzz, left-handed, less motion), the time zone of your phone (so daily rewards reset at your midnight) and your player name. The player name is made up by the game at random, for example "Brave Kite 482". You cannot type your own name. To pick a graphics level that runs smoothly, the game reads your phone's model and memory size on the phone itself. They are not saved with your progress, and neither is the graphics level you choose. Crash reports include the device model and memory size (see "Crash reports" below).

The save is kept on your phone and a copy is kept on our server. Changes you make in the game are sent to the server, which checks them against the game's rules and sends back the saved copy. This keeps your progress safe and stops cheating.

Your finished rounds

When a round ends, or you leave it part way, the game sends the round's details to our server: the round's starting number, the moments you tapped and where, and the result (score, XP, coins and why or when the round ended). The server plays the round again to check the score. We keep a record of each checked round (score, XP, coins, how it ended, the time and the game version).

Your player name and your best scores (all time and this week) are kept in a scores list on our server. The game's Top scores screen shows other players the top players' random names and best scores, and each player's own rank. It never shows any other detail. When a week ends, the top 10 players of that week can win coins in the game.

Your birth year

When you first open the game it asks "What year were you born?". We store the year and the age group it gives (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) in your save, on your phone and on our server. We use it only to decide:

  • whether ads can be offered (13 and over only),
  • whether a grown-up check is needed before a purchase (under 13),
  • whether offers for paid items are shown on the home screen (13 and over only).

We do not ask for your full date of birth and we do not check the answer against anything.

Purchases

When you buy coins or items, the purchase is made through the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google), under their terms and privacy policies. They handle your payment. We never receive your card, bank or billing details.

To give you what you bought, we use a purchase service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a record of the purchase from the store (the product, the time, a transaction id and your random player id) and tells our server. Our server keeps a record of which products were granted to your player id, so a purchase is never given twice.

Ads (players 13 and over only)

Updrift has rewarded ads only: you choose to watch an ad to keep going after a round, or to double the coins from a round. There are no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. You always get the same choice without an ad: say "No thanks".

Ads come from Google AdMob. The ad service is started only after the age question, and only for players 13 and over. Before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent step where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). If consent is not given where it is needed, no ad loads.

When the ad service runs, Google collects information to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. This can include your device's advertising id, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be worked out), and information about the ads you see and tap. Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy and at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We ask Google for ads suitable for everyone (rating G). For players aged 13 to 17 we tell Google that the player is under the age of consent, so Google limits how it uses their data for ads (for example, in Europe it shows non-personalised ads).

Players under 13

Players under 13 never see ads, and the ad service is never started for them. Before any real-money purchase, a grown-up check asks the person to type a number that is written in words.

For players under 13 we collect only what the game needs to work: the random player id, the game progress and rounds, the birth year, records of purchases made after the grown-up check, and crash reports. We do not use this information for advertising, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not build profiles from it.

If you are a parent or guardian and want to see or delete your child's information, use Settings, then Reset progress, on your child's device, or email us at francisvilaga@melioranexa.com.

Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report to Sentry, a service that helps us find and fix bugs. A report can include the device model and maker, its memory and storage (size and how much is free), screen size, battery level, language and time zone setting, the operating system version, the app version, the error and what the app was doing just before it, and a random id that Sentry makes for this installation of the game. It does not include your player id, your name or your IP address. We use crash reports only to fix problems.

Technical information our servers receive

Like any online service, our servers receive the IP address of each request and keep technical logs for security and to fix problems. Our server provider keeps these logs for a limited time. We do not use IP addresses to build profiles.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location, or messages. The game has no chat and no way to share content with other players.

We may share information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of players or others, or if Updrift is sold or transferred to someone who continues the game under this policy (we would tell you first).

Where your information is stored

Your saved progress, rounds, scores and purchase records are stored on servers in Tokyo, Japan. Requests to those servers may pass through our providers' networks in other countries. We are based in the Philippines. Where the law requires it, we use the safeguards our providers offer for international transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your save, rounds, scores and purchase records for as long as your player id exists. The round numbers our server gives out for checked rounds expire after 7 days, and expired ones are removed later.

We keep your data until you delete it, even if you stop playing. You can delete it at any time (see below).

When you delete your data (see below), we delete it from our database straight away. Copies in our providers' backups and logs disappear when those expire, within 30 days.

Deleting your data

In the game, open Settings, then Reset progress, then Reset everything. This:

  • clears all progress on your phone,
  • the next time your phone is online, deletes everything our server holds for your player id (your save, rounds, scores, round numbers and purchase records) and then deletes the player id itself.

The game then starts again as a new player. This cannot be undone.

Deleting your data does not cancel or refund a purchase. Records kept by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, Google AdMob and Sentry are kept under their own policies.

If you no longer have the game, or cannot use the button, email francisvilaga@melioranexa.com. Because we do not know who you are, deleting from inside the game is the surest way; by email we may need details from your device to find your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act, the EU and UK GDPR, or US state laws), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or limit how we use it, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us at francisvilaga@melioranexa.com and we will reply within 30 days.

The legal reasons we use your information are: to provide the game you asked for (progress, rounds, scores, purchases), our legitimate interest in keeping the game fair, secure and working (score checks, logs, crash reports), your consent where it is needed (ads), and legal obligations (purchase records).

Security

All traffic between the game and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Your sign-in key is kept in your phone's secure storage. Our database only lets players read their own save and rounds, and the scores list only through the game's Top scores screen; every change goes through our server, which checks it with the game's rules. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game before the change takes effect.
